Left the harbor around 6:15 AM and set up a trolling pattern running east to west along the 30ft contour. Fish were holding right on the mud-to-rock transition — the ones on pure mud were mostly drum, but anything near the harder bottom was walleye.

The chartreuse and orange Colorado blade harness was the clear winner. I ran a gold willow on the other side and it picked up a couple, but the Colorado outfished it at least 3 to 1. Speed was 1.2-1.4 mph — any faster and the bites dropped off.

Best fish came around 9 AM during a light rain. The bite slowed after 11 when the wind shifted more northerly and the waves picked up to 3-4ft. Called it around 1 PM.

Takeaway: when the water's stained like this, go loud on the blade color and keep the speed down. The walleye were feeding but not chasing.

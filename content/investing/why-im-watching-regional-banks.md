## The Setup

Regional banks have been out of favor since the SVB panic in early 2023. Even though the acute liquidity crisis passed quickly, the sector never fully recovered in terms of multiples. The KRE ETF is still well below its pre-crisis highs, and many individual names are trading below tangible book value.

What's changed is the rate environment. With the Fed likely at or near peak rates, banks that have been dealing with inverted yield curves and compressed net interest margins are starting to see relief. Q3 2025 earnings showed NIM expansion for the first time in several quarters at many regionals.

## What I'm Looking For

Before taking a position, I want to see:

1. **Deposit stabilization confirmed** — Two consecutive quarters of flat or growing deposits without relying on brokered CDs or promotional rates.
2. **CRE exposure clarity** — The commercial real estate overhang is the biggest risk. I need to see manageable LTV ratios and realistic reserve levels.
3. **Rate cut execution** — If the Fed actually delivers cuts, the gap between short-term funding costs and longer-term loan yields should widen. That's the trade.

## Risk

The main risk is a credit cycle that's worse than expected. If CRE defaults accelerate or consumer credit deteriorates sharply, even cheap valuations won't help. This is a "buy the hated sector" trade, which means it could stay hated for longer than I expect.

I'm staying patient. No position yet — just watching.

I've been keeping notes for years — fishing logs in a notebook, recipes on index cards, investing ideas in scattered Google Docs. This site is an attempt to put all of that in one place where I can find it later.

It's not a blog in the traditional sense. I'm not trying to build an audience or optimize for SEO. The `robots.txt` literally tells crawlers to go away. This is for me, and maybe for the handful of people I'd share a fishing report or recipe with.

The site is built with Hugo, hosted on GitHub Pages, and uses zero JavaScript. Pages load fast because there's nothing to load. The design is intentionally plain — I want to focus on the content, not the container.

I'll be adding posts as I go. Fishing logs after trips, recipes as I dial them in, investing notes as I think through positions. The portfolio section will have write-ups on projects I've built.

If you're reading this, you probably know me. Welcome.
